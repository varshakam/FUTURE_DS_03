# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a marketing campaign dataset to evaluate customer conversion performance and identify factors that influence successful marketing outcomes. The analysis helps businesses understand which communication channels, customer segments, and campaign periods perform best, enabling data-driven marketing decisions.

This project was completed as **Task 3** of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship (2026)**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall marketing campaign conversion rate.
- Identify the communication channel with the highest conversion rate.
- Compare conversion performance across different months.
- Identify customer segments with the highest conversion rates.
- Generate actionable business recommendations to improve future marketing campaigns.

---

## 📁 Dataset

**Dataset Used:** Bank Marketing Dataset (UCI Machine Learning Repository)

The dataset contains customer demographic information, campaign details, communication methods, and the final campaign outcome indicating whether a customer subscribed to the offered term deposit.

## 📈 Key Insights

- The overall marketing campaign conversion rate is approximately **11.70%**.
- Cellular communication achieved the highest customer conversion rate.
- March recorded the highest monthly conversion performance.
- Students and retired customers showed the highest likelihood of campaign conversion.
- Blue-collar customers demonstrated comparatively lower conversion rates.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize cellular communication for future marketing campaigns.
- Focus marketing efforts on customer groups with higher conversion potential.
- Increase campaign activities during high-performing months.
- Develop personalized marketing strategies for lower-converting customer segments.
